# SimpleScreen CRC

SimpleScreen CRC is Freenome's first screening test: a blood test for
colorectal cancer, and the company's first FDA-approved cancer screening
product.

## Freenome

Freenome is a biotechnology company that develops non-invasive, routine blood
tests designed to detect cancer at its earliest, most treatable stages. By
combining machine learning with multiomics — analyzing cell-free DNA, RNA,
proteins, and immune responses — its platform identifies subtle disease
patterns to screen for specific malignancies like colorectal cancer as well as
multiple cancer types. Freenome was my employer.

## The challenge

To define the workflows and take Freenome's first FDA-approved cancer screening
product from conception to a successful launch.

## What I did

I led the end-to-end experience strategy and design for SimpleScreen CRC. I
designed the entire ecosystem — an optimized, cohesive experience for every
user across every touchpoint, virtual and physical, right down to the paper
stock used on the labels.

That covered the digital platforms (the provider portal and lab operations) and
the physical touchpoints (kits, labeling, and reports).

Throughout the project I worked within the strict regulatory guidelines
required for FDA approval as a medical device, developing secure workflows that
safeguard sensitive information while following labeling requirements to the
letter.

## Impact

The project culminated in a successful launch of Freenome's first FDA-approved
cancer screening product.
